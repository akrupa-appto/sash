microphone access

sash needs a one-time permission grant to use your microphone for voice dictation in the side panel.

your voice goes straight to the AI provider you already configured with your own API key — OpenRouter, OpenAI, Gemini, or your own server — so it can be turned into text. it is never sent to a sash server, and sash does not record or store it.

chrome cannot show the permission prompt from the side panel itself, so this one full page exists to ask once. you can close this tab right after.
